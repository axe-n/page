```
Projects & Internships

```

Hierarchical Likelihood and PN Methods for SMBBH OJ287 Blazar — Masters Thesis
TIFR-M, Mumbai & IISER Thiruvananthapuram | 2026–Present | India
Working with Prof. A. Gopakumar and Dr. Abhimanyu Susobhanan on the OJ287 supermassive binary black hole system. The project focuses on post-Newtonian orbital dynamics, hierarchical likelihood methods, light-curve modelling of the central engine, and the possible connection between OJ287 and nanohertz gravitational-wave signals.

Multi-wavelength Study of Blazar Variability — Internship
Mt Abu Observatory, PRL Ahmedabad (Remote) | 2026–Present | India
Under the guidance of Prof. Sunil Chandra, this work involves CCD detector instrumentation, telescope systems, real-time AGN/blazar observations, data calibration using AstroImageJ, and Python-based photometric and light-curve analysis.

Modification of Ray-tracing Code for Testing GR near Non-Kerr Black Holes — Internship
Fudan University, Shanghai (Remote) | 2025 | China
Worked with Prof. Cosimo Bambi on modifying the BLACKRAY code to investigate relativistic spectra around non-Kerr black holes described by the Johannsen metric. The project strengthened my understanding of general relativity, accretion-disk models, relativistic calculations, and scientific programming.

Critical Behaviour of Cascading Networks — Project
IISER Thiruvananthapuram | 2025 | India
Conducted under Dr. Senthilkumar, this project explored concepts in network science and graph theory, leading to a potentially publishable result in Tuning robustness in spatial networks cascading failures.

Investigation on Halo Merger Histories — Internship
NIT Calicut | 2024 | India
Worked with Prof. Rajesh Mondal on exploring the ΛCDM cosmological model and studying the time evolution of dark-matter halos using C-based simulation codes. The project provided experience in computational cosmology, particle-mesh universe modelling, and the theoretical foundations of large-scale structure formation.
